🧭 Trap Visual Indications & Survival Guide
🧱 Crumbling Bridges

Blocks shake & flash amber when you step near them. Jump immediately without stopping!

👻 Hologram Fake Floors

Look like normal bridges but you fall right through! Look for upper ledges or hidden air blocks.

🟣 Purple Gravity Orbs

Flips gravity upside-down so you walk on the ceiling. Touch the second purple orb to flip back down.

🟠 Amber Mirror Orbs

Reverses LEFT and RIGHT controls! After touching an amber orb, press LEFT to move toward the right exit.

🔨 Piston Crushers

Heavy grey ceiling blocks with redstone cores. Step forward to bait the slam, step back, then sprint past.

🟢 Slime Bounce Pads

Check the ceiling above before bouncing! If spikes are overhead, jump around the slime pad instead.

💎 Bait Gems & Gold Switches

Floating gold gems & gold switches are traps! Only step on green Emerald switches to unlock portals.

🚪 Shy & Decoy Portals

Mid-stage portals are teleport traps! Real exit portals at the end may slide or hop when approached.
